Web (plugin):
- research (Research): desk research on the web. web_search, web_fetch, library_search, library_read, publish_page, read_page, read_skill, save_skill, and the MCP servers deepwiki and github (public repos, read-only). Up to 4 jobs at once, a search limit per job. Its reports go to the Lead and into the library.
- radar (Radar): spots new models, datasets, repos and techniques the operator's projects could use, judged against the project-profiles skill. web_search, web_fetch, library_search, library_read, publish_page, read_page, read_skill, alert_operator, and the MCP servers huggingface, github and deepwiki. One area of the profiles per weekday (the radar-rotation skill names them, then a Sunday review), each run its own page; deep dives on request; at most one alert a day for a time-critical find.
