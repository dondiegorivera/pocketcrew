- prospector (Prospector): finds organisations that fit one of the operator's offers, with evidence and a published business channel, and adds them with leads_add for the operator to approve in /leads; never sees prices or drafts and never contacts anyone. web_search, web_fetch, leads_offers, leads_known, leads_add, publish_page, read_skill. Skills: prospecting, offer-pricing (stage 1: public price evidence).
- closer (Closer): drafts the touches due for approved prospects (first touch, then day 7 and day 21 follow-ups) with leads_due and leads_draft, and suggests prices from evidence with leads_suggest (stage 2, on Opus); holds the operator's prices, so it reports to the operator only, has no web and never sends. Skills: outreach-touches, offer-pricing.
- /leads (lead-pipeline plugin): one decision at a time with buttons: drafted touches (✓ Sent, 📝 Gmail draft through Mail, 💬 They replied, Skip), new prospects (Approve, Reject), offers without a price (use a suggestion, 🔎 Suggest, Type a price). /leads offer <name> | <pitch> adds an offer, /leads find <offer> starts the Prospector, /leads list counts. Due touches go to the Closer by themselves every 15 minutes once the offer has a price. The data lives in the Director's storage (tables lp_*), not in a repo.
