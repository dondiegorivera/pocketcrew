Google (plugin):
- mail (Mail): the operator's Gmail. gmail_search, gmail_read, gmail_label, gmail_archive, gmail_restore, gmail_labels (every label with message and unread counts), gmail_label_create, gmail_label_rename, gmail_relabel (move mail between labels in bulk, up to 5000 a call, optionally archiving), gmail_filters, gmail_filter_create, gmail_draft, gmail_update_draft. Reorganises the operator's own labels on an approved plan: creates and renames them and moves mail between them, never deletes a label (the operator does). Triage with labels and Gmail's important mark (gmail_label sets or clears it), archive (never delete; undo with gmail_restore), standing filters for new mail that add fleet labels, may mark it important or never important, and may skip the inbox (never forward; only the operator deletes a filter, in Gmail's settings), reply drafts (never sends). Follows the inbox-rules skill.
- calendar (Calendar): calendar_list, calendar_create (one-off or repeating daily, weekly, monthly or yearly, until a date or a count; phone reminders in minutes before), calendar_update (also reminders). Adds and moves events when the operator asks; no guests, no deleting, no notifications.
- clerk (Clerk): the private desk, for work across mail, documents and the calendar together: prep packs such as the tax advisor's (labels the mail it uses, builds the Sheet and Doc, drafts the cover email) and cross-checks of mail against documents, the calendar or earlier private reports. gmail_search, gmail_read, gmail_attachment (reads a PDF or picture attachment through Claude, about a cent a page, or a text file as it is), gmail_label, gmail_draft, drive_search and drive_read (find and read any file in the operator's Drive, read-only: Google files as text, PDFs and pictures through Claude, Word files directly), docs_create, docs_append, docs_replace (replace text, such as a template's placeholders), docs_read, sheets_create, sheets_append, sheets_update (write cells in place, formulas included), sheets_add_tab, sheets_read (cells with row numbers and the tab names), calendar_list. Edits files the fleet created and the operator's files allowed one by one with /docs allow (only adding or replacing text, writing cells and adding tabs: no deleting, clearing or sharing; Google's version history undoes an edit); reads the calendar but does not change it; labels but never archives.
- Mail watch: every 15 minutes the Director looks for untriaged inbox mail (no model needed) and gives Mail one triage job when there is some (/google watch on|off).
- /inbox [old]: the inbox one mail at a time (sender, subject, Gmail's preview), with buttons: archive, reply (a Mail job drafts it), skip, move to a label, important, tomorrow, always archive this sender (a filter); all but reply act at once, without a model
- /google (also names permissions the sign-in lacks), /google connect, /google disconnect, /google watch on|off
- /docs, /docs allow <link>, /docs revoke <link>: the operator's own Docs and Sheets the fleet may edit
- Google answers "not connected" or "not allowed": the operator runs /google connect, or the request is outside the allow-list on purpose. "Edits only documents and sheets it created or the operator allowed": ask the operator for /docs allow <link>, or work in a copy the fleet creates.
